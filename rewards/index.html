FAQ

What do my referrals receive?

They get a complimentary EyeSTAR Suite stay or €50 off their surgery.

Do ineligible candidates count?

Yes. If medically ineligible, they count as 0.5 towards your total (max 2 ineligible referrals).

How is progress tracked?

Your referrals are recorded in our system. Check your progress using the Check My Progress button at the top of the page.

What if I’m not eligible?

Your reward can be transferred to someone you choose for up to 2 years.

How long does it take?

You have up to 2 years. If you refer fewer than 5, you can still use them as discounts (€150 per referral).

Can I transfer my reward?

Yes, you can gift or sell it, just inform us beforehand.
